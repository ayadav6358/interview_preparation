EN: Each vocabulary word is shown in 4 forms: Progresivo (romaji, used early on), Kana (hiragana/katakana — however the word is normally written), Kanji (where applicable), and the meaning. Hiragana+Katakana together are called "Kana."
HI: हर शब्द 4 रूपों में दिखाया गया है: रोमाजी, काना (हिरागाना/काताकाना), कांजी (जहाँ लागू हो), और अर्थ।
