Structure & Vocabulary

Apne domain ke phrases ready karna, connected sentences banana.

🔥 Warm-up (do this first)

10 min Shadowing (continue) — link neeche diya hai.

▶ Watch/Listen: BBC Learning English — 6 Minute English

🎯 Main Activity: Phrase List Building

  1. Apne kaam se juda 15 phrases likho jo aap meetings mein use karte ho (Phrase Bank dekho starter ke liye).
  2. Har phrase 3 baar zor se bolo.
  3. 3 phrases choose karo aur unse ek-ek sentence banao apne project ke baare mein.
✍️ Example — kya bolna hai Example phrase list to start with (copy + customize):
1. "I'd like to walk you through the current status."
2. "Let's dive into the main issue."
3. "We're currently on track with the delivery."
4. "There's a slight delay because of a dependency."
5. "I'll take this as an action item."

👉 Sentence example: "We're currently on track with the payment module — I'll take the remaining testing as an action item for this week."

🗣️ Today's Phrases (say each out loud 3x)

We're currently on track with...Status update dete waqt sab theek chal raha ho to.
There's a slight delay because...Delay explain karte waqt bina defensive hue.
The next step is to...Aage kya karna hai bataane ke liye.
We ran into an issue where...Problem report karte waqt neutral tone mein.
I'll circle back on that by [day].Commitment dene ka professional tareeka.
