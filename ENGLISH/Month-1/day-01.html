DAY 1 / 90 Month 1 · Week 1

Foundation & Comfort Building

Bolne ka darr kam karna, mouth muscles train karna, roz record karna.

🔥 Warm-up (do this first)

10 min Shadowing: Neeche diye video link se koi 1-2 min ka clip chuno. Pehle poora sun lo bina pause kiye. Phir dobara play karo, har sentence ke baad pause karo aur EXACTLY wahi bolo — tone, speed, stress copy karo.

▶ Watch/Listen: BBC Learning English — 6 Minute English

🎯 Main Activity: Speaking Practice: Self-Introduction

  1. Phone ka voice recorder kholo.
  2. 2 minute ka self-introduction record karo English mein — naam, role, experience, currently kya kaam kar rahe ho.
  3. Perfection ki chinta mat karo, bas bolna shuru karo aur poora karo.
  4. Recording ek baar sun lo — sirf 'kya main samajh pa raha hoon apni hi baat?' ye check karo, mistakes judge mat karo.
✍️ Example — kya bolna hai "Hi, my name is Anup. I have around 10 years of experience in the IT industry, mainly working as a [your role — e.g. backend developer]. Right now, I'm working on a project where we [1-line about current work — e.g. 'handle the payment system for an e-commerce platform']. Over the years, I've worked with technologies like [list 2-3 — e.g. Java, Spring Boot, AWS]. Outside of the technical side, I also [1 line — e.g. 'coordinate with the client team and review code for my juniors']. I'm currently working on improving my spoken English so I can communicate more confidently in meetings and presentations."

👉 Bas apne khaali jagah (role, project, tech) fill karo aur ise bolo — exact wording match karna zaroori nahi, ye sirf ek structure hai.

🗣️ Today's Phrases (say each out loud 3x)

I'd like to walk you through...Kisi topic ko step by step explain karne se pehle.
Let's dive into...Directly topic pe aane ke liye casual/confident opener.
Just to set context...Background dene se pehle bolo, taaki listener lost na ho.
Before we begin, I want to align on...Meeting shuru karne se pehle expectations set karna.
To kick things off...Presentation/discussion shuru karne ka friendly tareeka.

🪞 Reflection

Aaj bolte waqt sabse zyada hesitation kahan hua? (word choice / grammar / bas confidence)
