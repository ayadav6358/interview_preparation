▶ Watch/Listen: VOA Learning English (slow, clear pace)

🎯 Main Activity: Speaking Practice: Describe Your Daily Routine

  1. 2 minute record karo: subah uthne se lekar office/kaam shuru karne tak ka routine English mein bolo.
  2. Simple present tense use karo: 'I wake up at... I have breakfast... I start work at...'
  3. Recording sunkar note karo: kitni baar sentence beech mein ruka?
✍️ Example — kya bolna hai "I usually wake up at around 6:30 in the morning. After that, I freshen up and have breakfast with my family. I start my work by 9:30, and I usually begin the day by checking my emails and going through any pending messages from my team. Around 11, I have a stand-up call with my team where we discuss what everyone is working on. I take a short break for lunch around 1 o'clock, and then continue working till evening. After work, I like to relax by [watching something / going for a walk / spending time with family]."

👉 Isi structure ko apne actual routine ke hisaab se change kar do.

🗣️ Today's Phrases (say each out loud 3x)

Let's dive into...Directly topic pe aane ke liye casual/confident opener.
Just to set context...Background dene se pehle bolo, taaki listener lost na ho.
Before we begin, I want to align on...Meeting shuru karne se pehle expectations set karna.
To kick things off...Presentation/discussion shuru karne ka friendly tareeka.
