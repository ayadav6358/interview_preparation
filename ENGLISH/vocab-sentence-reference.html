Part 1

Office Vocabulary

Tasks & time

Deadline (डेडलाइन)date/time work must finishअंतिम तारीख़ / समय सीमा
"The deadline is this Friday."
Pending (पेंडिंग)not yet doneलंबित / बाकी
"Approval is still pending."
Prioritize (प्राय-ओ-रि-टाइज़)decide what's most important firstप्राथमिकता देना
"Let's prioritize the client issue."
On track (ऑन ट्रैक)progressing as plannedसही दिशा में / समय पर चल रहा
"The project is on track."
Bandwidth (बैंड-विड्थ)time/capacity for more workसमय / क्षमता
"I don't have the bandwidth today."
Milestone (माइल-स्टोन)an important point in a projectमहत्वपूर्ण पड़ाव
"We hit our first milestone."
Turnaround time (टर्न-अ-राउंड टाइम)how long a task takes to completeकाम पूरा होने में लगने वाला समय
"The turnaround time is 2 days."
Reschedule (री-शेड्यूल)change to a new timeसमय बदलना / दोबारा तय करना
"Can we reschedule the call?"

Problems & solutions

Blocker (ब्लॉ-कर)something stopping progressरुकावट
"The main blocker is the approval."
Escalate (एस-क-लेट)raise an issue to someone seniorबड़े अधिकारी तक बात पहुँचाना
"I'll escalate this to my manager."
Resolve (रि-ज़ॉल्व)fix a problemसुलझाना / हल करना
"We resolved the issue this morning."
Flag (फ्लैग)point out an issueकिसी बात की ओर इशारा करना
"I wanted to flag a concern."
Root cause (रूट कॉज़)the real reason behind a problemमूल कारण
"We found the root cause."
Workaround (वर्क-अ-राउंड)a temporary solutionअस्थायी हल
"We have a workaround for now."
Glitch (ग्लिच)a small technical errorछोटी तकनीकी खराबी
"There's a glitch in the system."

People & collaboration

Stakeholder (स्टेक-होल्डर)someone involved/affectedहितधारक (जुड़ा हुआ व्यक्ति)
"All stakeholders must sign off."
Sync up / Touch base (सिंक अप / टच बेस)have a short check-inमिलकर थोड़ी बात कर लेना
"Let's sync up after lunch."
Align (अ-लाइन)agree on the same planएक राय पर आना
"Let's align before the call."
Follow up (फॉलो अप)check on something laterबाद में जांच / याद दिलाना
"I'll follow up tomorrow."
Delegate (डे-लि-गेट)assign a task to someoneकाम सौंपना
"I'll delegate this to the team."
Onboard (ऑन-बोर्ड)help a new person get startedनए व्यक्ति को काम सिखाना
"We're onboarding a new hire."

Meetings & feedback

Agenda (अ-जेन-डा)list of topics for a meetingकार्यसूची
"Here's today's agenda."
Action item (एक्शन आइटम)a task decided in a meetingतय किया गया काम
"One action item: send the report."
Take the floor (टेक द फ्लोर)start speaking in a meetingबोलना शुरू करना
"I'll take the floor for 5 minutes."
Constructive feedback (कन्स्ट्रक्टिव फीड-बैक)helpful, specific criticismउपयोगी सुझाव
"Thanks for the constructive feedback."
Wrap up (रैप अप)finish/concludeसमाप्त करना
"Let's wrap up the meeting."
Part 2

Office Sentences

Starting & joining a meeting

"Good morning everyone, shall we get started?"
"Sorry I'm a little late, please continue."
"Can everyone hear me okay?"
"Let's go through today's agenda first."

Giving updates

"I'm currently working on the client report."
"This task is on track / running a bit behind."
"I should have this ready by Thursday."
"There's a small delay because I'm waiting on approval."
"Here's where things stand right now."

Asking for clarification

"Sorry, could you repeat that?"
"Could you clarify what you mean by 'urgent'?"
"Just to confirm, are you saying we push the deadline?"
"I'm not sure I follow — could you explain a bit more?"

Agreeing / disagreeing politely

"That makes sense, but I think we should also consider the timeline."
"I see your point, however I'd like to add one thing."
"Good point — I'd also suggest we test it first."
"I'm not fully convinced — can we discuss the risk here?"

Asking for help or leave

"Could you help me with this when you get a chance?"
"I'd like to take leave on Friday, is that okay?"
"I'm a bit stuck — could we look at this together?"
"Would you mind reviewing this before I send it?"

Apologizing & explaining a delay

"Apologies for the delay, I ran into an unexpected issue."
"Sorry for the late reply, I was in back-to-back meetings."
"I should have flagged this earlier, my mistake."

Emails & chat

"Just following up on this — any update?"
"Let me know if you need anything else."
"I'll get back to you by end of day."
"Attaching the file for your review."
"Looping in [name] for visibility."

Ending a meeting

"Let's wrap up — any final questions?"
"To summarize, here are the action items."
"Thanks everyone, talk soon."

Daily Life Vocabulary

Home & routine

Chores (चोर्स)small household tasksघर के छोटे काम
"I do my chores in the morning."
Errand (ए-रन्ड)a short task outside homeबाहर का छोटा काम
"I need to run an errand."
Leftovers (लेफ्ट-ओ-वर्स)food remaining from a mealबचा हुआ खाना
"We'll eat the leftovers tonight."
Doze off (डोज़ ऑफ)fall asleep brieflyझपकी लेना
"I dozed off on the couch."

Shopping & money

Afford (अ-फोर्ड)have enough money forखर्च उठा पाना
"I can't afford that right now."
Bargain / Haggle (बार्गन / हैगल)negotiate a lower priceमोल-भाव करना
"I haggled at the market."
Refund (री-फंड)money returned for a returnपैसे वापस मिलना
"I asked for a refund."
Worth it (वर्थ इट)good value for the price/effortकीमत के लायक
"It's expensive but worth it."

Travel & transport

Commute (कम्-यूट)travel to work regularlyरोज़ाना काम पर आना-जाना
"My commute takes an hour."
Stuck in traffic (स्टक इन ट्रैफिक)delayed by heavy trafficट्रैफिक में फंसना
"I'm stuck in traffic, running late."
Detour (डी-टूर)a longer, alternate routeघुमावदार वैकल्पिक रास्ता
"We took a detour to avoid traffic."
Fare (फेयर)price of a ride/ticketकिराया
"What's the fare to the station?"

Food & restaurants

Craving (क्रे-विंग)a strong desire for a foodखाने की तीव्र इच्छा
"I have a craving for pizza."
Bland (ब्लैंड)lacking strong flavorबेस्वाद / फीका
"This dish tastes a bit bland."
Portion (पोर-शन)amount of food servedखाने की मात्रा
"The portion size was huge."
Take-out / Parcel (टेक-आउट / पार्सल)food packed to carry homeपैक करवाया हुआ खाना
"Let's get take-out tonight."

Feelings & small talk

Exhausted (इग्-ज़ॉस-टेड)very tiredबहुत थका हुआ
"I'm exhausted after work."
Overwhelmed (ओ-वर-वेल्म्ड)too much to handle at onceबहुत ज़्यादा दबाव महसूस करना
"I feel overwhelmed with tasks."
Looking forward to (लुकिंग फॉर-वर्ड टू)excited about something comingबेसब्री से इंतज़ार करना
"I'm looking forward to the weekend."
In a good/bad mood (इन अ गुड/बैड मूड)feeling good/bad generallyअच्छे / बुरे मूड में होना
"She's in a good mood today."

Health

Under the weather (अंडर द वे-दर)feeling slightly unwellथोड़ा अस्वस्थ महसूस करना
"I'm feeling under the weather."
Come down with (कम डाउन विद)start to have an illnessबीमार पड़ने लगना
"I think I'm coming down with a cold."
Recover (रि-क-वर)get better after illnessठीक होना
"I'm recovering from the flu."
Part 4

Daily Life Sentences

Morning & routine

"I woke up a bit late today."
"I need to grab breakfast before I leave."
"Traffic is heavy this morning, I might be a few minutes late."

Making plans with friends

"Are you free this weekend?"
"Let's catch up over coffee sometime."
"What time works for you?"
"I'll text you once I'm on my way."

At a restaurant

"Could we get a table for two, please?"
"Could I see the menu, please?"
"I'll have the same as him/her."
"Could we get the bill, please?"

Shopping

"Do you have this in a different size?"
"Is there any discount on this?"
"Can I pay by card?"
"I'd like to return this, it doesn't fit."

Asking for directions

"Excuse me, how do I get to the metro station?"
"Is it within walking distance?"
"Could you point me in the right direction?"

Talking about your day / feelings

"It's been a long day, I'm quite tired."
"I'm a little stressed about the deadline tomorrow."
"I'm really looking forward to the weekend."
"Honestly, today went better than I expected."

Phone calls

"Hello, is this a good time to talk?"
"Sorry, the line's a bit unclear, could you repeat that?"
"I'll call you back in a few minutes."
