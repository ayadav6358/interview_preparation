Observability Concepts & Golden Signals

Basic Monitoring, Logging & Observability

Concept (What & Why)

Monitoring 'kya toota' batati hai, observability 'kyun toota'. Three pillars: metrics (numbers), logs (events), traces (request path).

Definition Deep-Dive

QuestionAnswer
Simple definitionObservability means you can understand what is happening inside a system by looking at the data it produces.
Technical definitionObservability is the ability to infer internal state from outputs: metrics, logs, traces (and events/profiles), enabling investigation of unknown failure modes. The golden signals summarise service health.
Why does it exist?Distributed systems fail in new ways.
What happens without it?Guessing during incidents.
When to useAll production systems.
When NOT to useCollecting everything without purpose (cost).
Real-world analogyA car dashboard plus black-box recorder.

Diagram & Internal Working

Metrics: CPU 80%, req/s, p99 latency   -> Prometheus
Logs   : 'ERROR db timeout user=42'    -> Loki / ELK
Traces : gateway -> api -> db (120ms)  -> Jaeger / Tempo
Golden signals: Latency | Traffic | Errors | Saturation
RED (services): Rate, Errors, Duration ; USE (resources): Utilization, Saturation, Errors

How it works internally (diagram explained)

Each request generates telemetry: counters and timers (metrics), events (logs) and spans (traces) that share identifiers. Backends store them, Grafana correlates them: a metric spike leads to related logs by labels/time and to a trace by exemplar/trace_id.

Real-World Example

checkout API: Latency p95 up (metric) -> errors in payment-service logs -> trace shows 2s DB span

An e-commerce checkout slows. The RED dashboard shows p95 latency rising, logs from the payment service show timeouts, and one trace reveals a database call taking two seconds because of a missing index. You go from symptom to cause in minutes.

Practical Examples

Design alert philosophy

Alert on SYMPTOMS (user pain: error rate, latency), not causes (CPU 80%).
Every page must be actionable + have a runbook.
Severity: page (wake up) vs ticket (next day) vs info.

Lab Goal

Explore golden signals with a demo app.

Prerequisites

  • Docker

Step 1

Example app exposing /metrics.

Step 2

curl -s localhost:8080/metrics | head -20

You will see counters and histograms in Prometheus text format.

for i in $(seq 1 50); do curl -s localhost:8080/ >/dev/null; done; curl -s localhost:8080/metrics | grep http_requests_total

Counter value increased.

Real-World Problems & Fixes (Troubleshooting)

Problem: Alert fatigue: team ignores pages
Cause: Too many noisy cause-based alerts
Fix: Alert on SLO burn rate, delete non-actionable alerts, group/route in Alertmanager.

Interview Questions

Q1. Monitoring vs observability?

Monitoring watches known failure modes; observability lets you ask new questions from telemetry.

Q2. Four golden signals?

Latency, traffic, errors, saturation.

Q3. What is an SLI/SLO/SLA?

SLI measured indicator; SLO target; SLA contractual promise with penalty.

Q4. Why p99 not average latency?

Averages hide tail latency affecting real users.

Q5. Black-box vs white-box monitoring?

Black-box tests from outside (probes); white-box uses internal metrics.

Q6. Why percentiles instead of averages?

Averages hide slow tail requests; p95/p99 show what unlucky users experience.

Why: Metric literacy.

Real-world example: Average 200ms but p99 5s.
